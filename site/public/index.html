QW Privacy Support

An AI that never goes online.

Qwen3.5‑0.8B runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad. The model ships inside the app.

What it is

A 0.8‑billion‑parameter language model is installed along with the app. There is nothing to download afterwards, no account to create, and no network request of any kind. Turn on Airplane Mode and everything still works, because it never needed the connection in the first place.

It looks like a notepad rather than a chat app. What you write and what the model answers live in one continuous page, top to bottom. Put the keyboard away and the text sets in place; the answer starts on the next line.

Where your writing goes

Nowhere. Notes are kept in a file inside the app's own container on the device. They are not synced, not uploaded, and not readable by other apps. Deleting QW deletes them with it.

This is not a policy decision that could quietly change in an update — the app contains no networking code for it to change into. See the privacy policy for the full accounting.

Requirements

  • iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later
  • About 600 MB of free space — most of it is the model
  • A few seconds on first launch while the weights are mapped into memory

Built on

Qwen3.5‑0.8B under Apache‑2.0, with inference by llama.cpp (MIT) on the Metal backend. The app itself is open source at github.com/argoodies/qw.
